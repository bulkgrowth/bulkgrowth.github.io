FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How do you track WhatsApp leads?

We track every WhatsApp click, every conversation that actually starts, and which ad or campaign started it. When a chat turns into a real lead or a sale, that is sent back to Meta so your ads learn to bring more customers like that one.

Why does tracking matter before running ads?

Ad platforms optimize on the results you report to them. If sales, leads and WhatsApp chats are not tracked correctly, they optimize for clicks instead of customers. We connect your site, store, WhatsApp and app to Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat and GA4 first, so every campaign learns from real results.

Can I advertise on ChatGPT?

Yes, in the markets where OpenAI has opened ads. ChatGPT shows sponsored placements next to relevant answers. We set up and manage ChatGPT Ads and tell you upfront if your market isn't available yet.

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in Google results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand, trust and recommend your brand. The groundwork overlaps, so we run both together.

Which industries do you work with?

E-commerce and retail, medical and aesthetic clinics, real estate and construction, restaurants and cafes, tourism and hospitality, and SaaS.

Which markets do you work in?

Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait and Egypt, plus campaigns in the USA, UK, Germany, France and other European countries, in English and Arabic.

Who owns the ad accounts and the tracking setup?

You do. Ad accounts, tracking and analytics stay in your business, and we work in them with access permissions.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with brands in the GCC, Egypt, Europe and the USA, in English and Arabic. Communication runs on WhatsApp and video calls, and reporting is shared through a live dashboard.

How do you measure success?

We agree on business numbers before launch: return on ad spend and cost per purchase for online stores, cost per qualified lead and closed deals for lead generation. Clicks and reach are tracked, but they are not the goal.

What happens in the $50 consultation?

It is a 30-minute video call about your business. You answer a short form and pick a time in our calendar, we review your ads, funnel and tracking before the call, and in the call we show you the biggest problems costing you money and the quickest fixes. You get a short written summary afterwards.

How do I pay for the consultation?

After you pick a time in our calendar, we send you a secure invoice for $50 on WhatsApp. You can pay it by bank transfer in USD (ACH or wire) or EUR (SEPA) from anywhere. The call is booked once the payment arrives.

How does pricing work?

A monthly management fee based on ad spend, channels and services. Tracking setups are quoted as a one-time project. Message us on WhatsApp for a detailed quote.
