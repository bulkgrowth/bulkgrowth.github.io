Introduction

Smart data. Strategic ads. Scalable growth.

Hi, I'm Mohamed Elmlegey, a Performance Marketer and Media Buyer, founder of MEGO and @bulk.growth. I help brands turn ad spend into real, measurable growth.

Since 2020 I have managed campaigns worth millions for clinics, real-estate projects, construction and finishing companies, restaurants, e-commerce stores, hotels, travel companies and SaaS platforms, in Qatar, the GCC, Egypt, the USA and Europe.

Industry 01 · Qatar & GCC

Medical & Aesthetic Clinics

My largest vertical. Clinics, med-spas and doctors' personal brands selling botox, filler, laser, dental, hydrafacial, PRP and cosmetic procedures. The goal on almost every account is the same: start a WhatsApp or Messenger conversation with a real patient at a cost the clinic can scale, then hand it to the reception team to book.

How I ran it

  • Full funnel: Awareness → Traffic / profile visits → Messaging campaigns, with retargeting of engaged audiences
  • Creative testing around doctor personal branding, treatment offers, before/after and educational hooks
  • Winners scaled with CBO; messaging, lead-form and Snapchat instant-form objectives run side by side
  • Clinics report closing rates of 50% to 60% on these leads (up to 80% from messaging to booked appointment)
