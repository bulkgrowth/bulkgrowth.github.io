Bulk Growth is a performance marketing agency for e-commerce. We fix purchase tracking, build campaigns around your best products and offers, test creatives weekly and scale budget while watching profit, not just ROAS.

The problems we usually find

  • Purchases counted twice, or not at all, so ROAS in the ad platform doesn't match your bank.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders that are cancelled or returned but still counted as sales.
  • Budgets spread across too many campaigns to learn.
  • Creatives that stopped working weeks ago.

How we grow online stores

  1. Tracking: purchase value, deduplication and product IDs that match the catalog. See tracking.
  2. Offer and product focus: find the products and bundles that sell profitably and lead with them.
  3. Channel mix: Meta and TikTok/Snapchat to create demand, Google Shopping and Search to capture it.
  4. Creative testing every week, with retargeting for visitors who didn't buy.
  5. Scaling in steps while checking contribution margin.

Platforms we work with

Shopify, Salla, Zid, WooCommerce and custom stores.

Why ROAS alone can mislead

A high ROAS on a low-margin product can still lose money. We look at cost per purchase against your margin, repeat purchases and returns. Read how to split budget between Meta and Google.

Frequently asked questions

What ROAS should my store target?

It depends on your margins. We calculate your break-even ROAS from product cost, shipping, returns and fees, then set targets above it.

Do you work with cash-on-delivery stores?

Yes. We adjust reporting for cancelled and returned COD orders so decisions are based on collected revenue.
