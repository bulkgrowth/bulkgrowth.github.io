Bulk Growth manages Google Ads for e-commerce brands and service businesses. We target high-intent searches, set up Shopping and Performance Max with a clean product feed, track calls and leads as conversions, and adjust bids based on real sales rather than clicks.

What we do

Search campaigns

We build keyword groups around buying intent ("hire", "price", "near me", product names) and keep a negative keyword list that blocks irrelevant searches. Ads and landing pages match the search, which raises quality and lowers cost per click.

Shopping and Performance Max

For online stores we clean the Merchant Center feed (titles, categories, images, prices) because the feed decides when your products show. Performance Max is used with clear asset groups and audience signals, and checked against Search so it doesn't take credit for branded traffic.

Lead and call tracking

For service businesses we track form submissions, calls and WhatsApp clicks, and import qualified leads back into Google Ads so bidding learns from the leads that turn into customers. See conversion tracking.

What you get

  • A search term and wasted-spend audit.
  • A rebuilt campaign and keyword structure.
  • Enhanced conversions and offline lead import where possible.
  • A monthly report on cost per lead, cost per sale and ROAS.

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Ads or Meta Ads better for my business?

It depends on whether people already search for your product. Google captures existing demand; Meta creates new demand. Many businesses need both. Read Meta Ads vs Google Ads.

Do you manage Performance Max?

Yes, with separate asset groups, audience signals and checks that it isn't just capturing branded searches you would get anyway.
