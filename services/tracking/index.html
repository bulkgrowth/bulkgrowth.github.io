Tracking & analytics

Conversion tracking you can trust

We connect your store, forms, WhatsApp and app to Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat and GA4, so every sale and lead is counted once and sent back to the ads that brought it.

Conversion tracking means recording the actions that matter to your business (purchases, leads, WhatsApp chats, app installs) and sending them to your ad platforms and analytics. Bulk Growth sets it up with Google Tag Manager, GA4 and each platform's Conversions API, then tests every event before launch.

Why tracking comes first

Ad platforms optimize toward the signals you send them. If purchases aren't recorded, or are counted twice, the algorithm learns the wrong thing and your cost per result rises. Fixing tracking is often the fastest win in an account.

What we track

BusinessActions we track
Online storesProduct views, add to cart, checkout, purchases with value, refunds and cancelled cash-on-delivery orders
Lead generationForm starts and submissions, booked calls, instant-form leads, qualified leads and closed deals from your CRM
WhatsAppButton clicks, conversations started, the campaign that started each chat, chats that became leads or sales
Mobile appsInstalls, sign-ups, in-app purchases, trials and subscriptions, by campaign

What we connect

Google Tag Manager, GA4, Meta Pixel and Conversions API, Google Ads enhanced conversions, TikTok Events API, Snapchat Conversions API, your CRM and a Looker Studio dashboard.

What you get

  • A tracking plan in plain language.
  • The full setup inside your own accounts.
  • Testing in GTM Preview, GA4 DebugView and each platform's event tester.
  • A live dashboard with spend, leads, sales and ROAS.

Frequently asked questions

Can you track WhatsApp leads from ads?

Yes. We track clicks and conversations, tag each chat with its source, and send qualified leads and sales back to Meta. Details in our WhatsApp tracking guide.

Do you set up server-side tracking?

Yes, when the business benefits from it. Server-side Tag Manager and Conversions APIs make tracking more resilient to browser restrictions.

How long does a tracking setup take?

A typical store or lead-gen site takes one to two weeks including testing. Apps take longer because of SDK and app store releases.
