Meta Ads agency

Meta Ads management that pays back

We plan, run and scale Facebook and Instagram Ads for online stores, clinics and service businesses, with tracking set up first so Meta optimizes for real customers.

Bulk Growth is a Meta Ads agency that manages Facebook and Instagram campaigns for e-commerce and lead-generation businesses. We fix tracking first (Pixel and Conversions API), structure the account around one business goal, test creatives every week and move budget to what brings profitable sales or qualified leads.

When you need a Meta Ads agency

  • Your cost per purchase or per lead keeps rising and you don't know why.
  • You get many messages or form fills, but few of them buy.
  • Results drop every time you try to raise the budget.
  • You can't tell which ads actually bring sales.

What we do

1. Tracking before spend

We check the Meta Pixel, Conversions API and event deduplication, and make sure purchases, leads and WhatsApp chats are sent back to Meta with the right value. Meta can only optimize for what it can see. See conversion tracking.

2. Account structure

We simplify campaigns around one goal: sales, leads or messages. Fewer, larger ad sets give the algorithm enough data to learn. Advantage+ campaigns are used where the catalog and data support them.

3. Creative testing

Creative is the main lever on Meta today. We test new hooks, offers and formats (UGC, static, short video) on a weekly cycle and keep a log of what wins and why.

4. Scaling with margin

Budget moves to winning campaigns in steps, while we watch cost per result and lead quality. Retargeting and lookalike audiences recover warm visitors.

What you get

  • An account audit with the main leaks, ranked by cost.
  • A clean campaign structure and naming system.
  • A weekly creative test plan.
  • A live dashboard with spend, results, cost per result and ROAS.

Who it's for

E-commerce brands, medical and aesthetic clinics, real estate and service businesses in the GCC, Europe and the USA, in English and Arabic.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on Meta Ads to start?

Enough to give each campaign a steady flow of results to learn from. We set the starting budget after reviewing your product, price and market in the consultation, rather than using a fixed number.

Do you work with click-to-WhatsApp ads?

Yes. We run messaging campaigns and track which chats become real leads or sales, so Meta learns from quality conversations. See how to track WhatsApp leads.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your Business Manager with partner access, so data and history stay with you.
