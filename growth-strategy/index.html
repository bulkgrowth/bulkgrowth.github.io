How we build a growth strategy for every client

Ads only work when the plan behind them is right. Every client starts with market research, a competitor audit and a written plan with clear targets.

A growth strategy is a written plan that defines the goal, studies the market and competitors, sets the budget split and content direction, and fixes the KPIs we will be judged on. We write one for every client before launch and update it every month from real results.

The 8 parts of every plan

Before we spend a riyal, dirham or pound, every client gets a written plan. It always covers the same eight parts, so nothing is left to guesswork.

  1. Objectives and success metrics. One main goal (sales, leads, bookings or store visits) and the numbers that prove it.
  2. Competitive analysis. We audit the top competitors: followers, posting frequency, content types, video views, engagement and how they position themselves. The gaps become our opportunities.
  3. Media plan. Budget split by funnel stage and platform, ad formats, targeting and a phase-by-phase calendar.
  4. Content direction. Tone of voice, visual style, hooks and references: UGC, doctor or founder videos, product shots, AI-assisted video.
  5. Platforms. Where the buyers actually are: Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, Google, LinkedIn or ChatGPT Ads.
  6. Audiences and personas. Who buys, why, and what stops them, written as two or three real profiles.
  7. Messaging and value proposition. The one reason to choose the client over everyone else, and the angles we test.
  8. KPIs and measurement plan. Targets per funnel stage, the tracking needed to measure them, and the rules for what we change when a number drops.

Home furnishings · 15 branches · Cairo, Egypt

Elkhawaga Furnishings

  • Goal: Drive more customers to 15 branches across Cairo through awareness and geo-targeted campaigns (3-month plan, Nov 2025 – Feb 2026).
  • Market analysis: Audited six competitors (Naguib Selim, Shatta, Raif, Capitonner, Radwan Mardini, SASA) on followers, posting frequency, content type and video views.
  • Insight: Elkhawaga was close to the top tier in followers but behind on engagement: daily posting, no consistent tone. We shifted the content toward storytelling and real customer stories.
  • Targets: 5M+ impressions, 6%+ engagement, CTR 1.5%+ on Meta and 2%+ on TikTok, +20% store visits (QR and POS), +15% followers.

MEP, HVAC & fire-fighting contractor · Jeddah, Saudi Arabia

Green Flash

  • Goal: Position the company as a premium MEP contractor and generate B2B leads from developers, consultants and facility managers.
  • Market analysis: Most competitors win through tenders and relationships and are weak online: no technical content, no case studies, little retargeting or LinkedIn. A clear gap to own.
  • Plan: Technical video content, LinkedIn, Google Search and TikTok, landing pages and a CRM, with Pixel, Conversions API and UTMs tracking 95%+ of events.
  • Targets: 80 to 200 leads in 3 months at 50 to 150 SAR each, 30%+ video completion, 0.5%+ CTR, 90%+ sales follow-up.
