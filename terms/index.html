Terms of service

The rules that apply when you work with Bulk Growth. Please read them before you book or sign.

In short: management services run on a minimum 12-month agreement, all fees are paid in advance, payments are non-refundable, and ending the agreement early carries an early termination fee as set out in your signed agreement.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are

Bulk Growth ("we", "us") is a performance marketing agency led by Mohamed Elmlegey. These terms apply to every service we provide, including consultations, ad management, tracking setup, SEO and GEO, content and strategy work. If a signed service agreement or proposal says something different, the signed document wins.

2. Minimum term: 12 months

  • Ongoing services (ad management, page management, SEO and GEO, retainers) are sold on a minimum agreement of 12 months from the start date.
  • After the first 12 months the agreement renews for another 12 months unless either side gives written notice at least 30 days before the end of the term.
  • One-off work (a consultation, a tracking setup, a strategy document) has no minimum term and ends when the work is delivered.

3. Payment in advance

  • All fees are paid in advance. Monthly fees are due before the start of each service month, or upfront for the period stated in your agreement.
  • Work starts, and continues, only after payment is received. If a payment is late, we may pause the work until it is paid; paused time still counts toward the term.
  • We invoice in USD or EUR. Payment is by bank transfer or another method stated on the invoice. Bank and transfer fees are paid by the client.
  • The 30-minute consultation ($50) is paid before the call.

4. No refunds

Because we reserve time, people and planning for you as soon as you pay, all payments are final and non-refundable, including consultation fees, setup fees and monthly fees, whether or not the services are used in full. See our refund policy for details.

5. Ending the agreement early: termination fee

  • You can end the agreement before the 12-month term ends by giving written notice.
  • Ending early carries an early termination fee. The amount is stated in your signed service agreement.
  • Fees already paid are not refunded, and any unpaid fees for work already done stay due.
  • We may end the agreement immediately if fees remain unpaid, or if the client breaks these terms or the ad platforms' policies. In that case the early termination fee still applies.

6. Ad spend

Our fees do not include the money spent on ads. Ad spend is paid by the client directly to Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, OpenAI or any other platform, using the client's own payment method. We are not responsible for platform charges, taxes, currency fees or billing errors made by the platforms.

7. Results

We work to improve your results and report them honestly, but advertising depends on many things outside our control: your offer, price, product, website, sales team, competition and platform algorithms. We do not guarantee specific results, such as a set number of leads, sales or a return on ad spend. Past results shown on this website do not promise future results.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Give us the access we need (ad accounts, Business Manager, website, tag manager) on time.
  • Provide accurate information, approve content and creative on time, and reply to leads quickly.
  • Make sure your products, services, claims and content are legal and follow each platform's advertising policies.

Delays caused by missing access, content or approvals do not extend the term or reduce the fees.

9. Accounts, data and ownership

  • Ad accounts, pixels, data and pages stay in the client's name. We work through partner or user access.
  • Creative and content we produce for you become yours once all related fees are paid. We may show anonymised results and work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to in writing.
  • Plans, frameworks and internal methods remain ours.

10. Platform decisions

Ad platforms can reject ads, restrict or disable accounts at their own discretion. We will help you appeal and comply, but we are not responsible for platform decisions, and they do not stop or reduce the fees.

11. Confidentiality

Both sides keep the other's private business information confidential, during the agreement and after it ends.

12. Limitation of liability

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profit, lost sales or lost data, and our total liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the three months before the claim.
