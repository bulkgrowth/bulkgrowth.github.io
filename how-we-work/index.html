How we work

Tracking first, a clear funnel, then weekly testing and scaling.

Process

Four steps, each with a clear output

Audit

Ad accounts, tracking, funnel and competitors reviewed. You get a list of leaks and opportunities.

Track & plan

Tracking fixed first. Then budget split, audiences, offers, and agreed ROAS, CPA or CPL targets.

Launch & test

Campaigns and creatives go live with structured tests to find winning hooks and offers fast.

Scale

More budget on winners, retargeting and upsell, then new channels or markets when the numbers allow.

About us

A paid ads company that builds systems, not campaigns

Bulk Growth is a company specialized in paid advertising. We plan, run and scale ads on Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn and ChatGPT for brands that want sales and leads they can measure.

We don't launch ads and hope. We build a full system around every account: the right tracking first, then a clear funnel, then campaigns that are tested and optimized every week. Tracking and optimization are the core of everything we do, because they decide whether an ad budget grows a business or burns it.

Since 2022 we've helped 40+ brands across the GCC, Europe and the USA, with campaigns on Meta, Google, TikTok and Snapchat. Our clients include online stores, medical and aesthetic clinics, real estate developers, restaurants and cafés, hotels and travel companies, construction firms and SaaS platforms.

For them we've built full-funnel systems from first view to repeat purchase, set up tracking that connects every sale, lead and WhatsApp chat back to the ad that brought it, and scaled the winners. The results include 245,000+ conversations and leads, a 6.1x ROAS on EGP 10.6M of tracked e-commerce sales, a 12.5x return for a real estate developer and 74,840 patient conversations at 4.66 QAR for one clinic group, all shown with screenshots on our results page.

Bulk Growth is led by Mohamed Elmlegey, a senior media buyer and performance marketer who has managed paid media since 2022 and mentored teams of media buyers.
