About Bulk Growth

A paid ads company that builds systems, not campaigns. Tracking first, constant optimization, and growth you can measure.

Bulk Growth is a performance marketing agency specialized in paid advertising on Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn and ChatGPT, with conversion tracking and AI search visibility. It is led by Mohamed Elmlegey, a senior media buyer and performance marketer who has managed paid media since 2022.

What we believe

Ads don't fail because of the platform. They fail because tracking is broken, the funnel leaks or no one tests creatives. We fix those first, then scale.

How we work

  • Tracking first: every sale, lead and WhatsApp chat counted before we spend.
  • Weekly optimization: budgets, audiences and creatives reviewed every week.
  • Systems that scale: a full funnel from first view to repeat purchase.
  • Transparency: ad accounts and data stay in your name.

Who leads the agency

Mohamed Elmlegey is a senior media buyer and performance marketer. He has managed campaigns for e-commerce brands, medical and aesthetic clinics, real estate developers, restaurants, hotels and SaaS companies, and has led and mentored teams of media buyers. His focus is data, conversion funnels and steady optimization.

Where we work

We work remotely with clients across the GCC, Europe and the USA, in English and Arabic. Communication runs on WhatsApp and video calls.

Experience

40+ brands across clinics, e-commerce, construction, real estate, tourism and SaaS. 245,000+ conversations and leads, a 6.1x e-commerce ROAS and a 12.5x real estate return, all with screenshots on our results page.
