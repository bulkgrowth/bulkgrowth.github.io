Result: 26,249 Meta conversations at 19.42 QAR, 4,133 TikTok conversations at 13.92 QAR, 790 more on the doctor's own TikTok at 11.25 QAR and 410 Snapchat leads. Between March and July 2026 the TikTok cost per lead fell from 16.27 to 8.61 QAR and Snapchat from $6.44 to $4.42.

About the client

Alnawras is a medical and aesthetic center in Doha offering cosmetic treatments such as fillers, Botox, lip and nose procedures and body contouring, with several doctors on staff. Most patients in Qatar ask on WhatsApp before booking, so every campaign is built to start a conversation with the clinic.

The challenge

Meta was the main source of patients, but the cost per conversation climbed as budgets grew. The center needed more inquiries without paying more for each one.

What we did

  • Meta as the base: one long-running messaging campaign plus monthly offer campaigns per treatment. The biggest campaign has delivered 14,100 conversations at 18.76 QAR on 263,700 QAR of spend.
  • TikTok with native video: doctor-led clips and results-style hooks within platform rules, 128 ads tested.
  • Snapchat lead forms for younger Gulf audiences.
  • A personal account for Dr Hala, so her own audience books with the clinic.
  • Monthly reports and a "winner creative" per platform, so each month starts from what worked.
