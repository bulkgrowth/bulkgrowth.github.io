Result: the clinic account brought 26,579 Meta conversations, three doctor accounts added 6,261 more, TikTok added 5,086 and Snapchat lead forms brought 855 leads. The clinic reports closing up to 60% of Snapchat leads and 50% of TikTok leads.

The idea

In aesthetics, patients follow doctors. Alongside the clinic's main account, we ran separate personal-brand ad accounts for each doctor, so each doctor's face and expertise could build its own audience and send patients to the same clinic.

What we did

  • Clinic account: always-on messaging campaigns plus a "winners" campaign that only ran proven creatives.
  • Doctor accounts: short videos from each doctor about their treatments, optimized for WhatsApp conversations.
  • TikTok and Snapchat added with native creative and lead forms.

Getting better month by month

Between December 2025 and March 2026, the clinic's monthly Meta conversations grew from 1,570 to 3,592 (+129%), while the cost per conversation fell from 22.60 to 16.29 QAR (−28%). That came from a content plan built around the ads: scripts per service, doctor reels, UGC and AI-assisted videos, tested and refreshed every month.

The best clinic campaign, built only from winning creatives, brought 1,415 conversations at 13.97 QAR, about 37% below the account average.

Takeaway

A doctor's own account often brings cheaper conversations than the clinic's. Doctor 1 reached 13.88 QAR on Meta and 12.56 QAR on TikTok, both well below the clinic account.
