The challenge

Construction services are expensive and bought by people with a project in hand. Cheap clicks don't help. The goal was serious inquiries at a cost that makes sense for a high-ticket sale.

What we did

  • A multi-stage funnel: awareness video, then messaging and lead campaigns to people who watched.
  • A/B tests on copy and creative: project photos, site videos and facade before-and-afters.
  • Targeting focused on business owners, project managers and homeowners.
  • TikTok added once Meta was stable, with native site videos.
