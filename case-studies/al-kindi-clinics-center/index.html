Result: two Meta accounts spent 348,388 QAR and received 74,840 WhatsApp and Messenger conversations at an average of 4.66 QAR. Treatment campaigns went as low as 0.60 QAR per conversation.

About the client

Al Kindi Clinics Center offers dental packages, dermatology and laser treatments in Qatar. Its aesthetic brand, Purple Rose, offers HydraFacial, PRP, Botox, Dermapen, carbon laser and tattoo removal.

What we did

  • One campaign per treatment, each with its own offer and creative, so budget follows demand.
  • Monthly treatment offers on lifetime budgets, with always-on services on daily budgets.
  • Creative testing across doctor videos, package posts and promotional designs in Arabic and English.
  • Retargeting people who engaged but didn't message.

How we improved it

Splitting by treatment showed which services patients actually ask about. Laser and tattoo-removal campaigns brought conversations for under 2 QAR, while dental packages brought volume. Budget moves every month to the cheapest treatments.
