Result: between December 2025 and March 2026, monthly Meta conversations grew from 1,570 to 3,592 (+129%) while the cost per conversation dropped from 22.60 to 16.29 QAR (−28%). Across the clinic and three doctor accounts we have delivered 38,843 patient conversations and leads.

About the client

Bella Paris is a medical and aesthetic center in Doha. Its doctors, including Dr Enas, Dr Feras and Dr Yasser, each have their own following, and patients often ask for a doctor by name.

The challenge

The clinic's own account was paying over 22 QAR per conversation, and all doctors were promoted from one account, so no doctor built an audience of their own.

What we did

  • Separate personal-brand accounts for each doctor on Meta and TikTok, all sending patients to the same clinic WhatsApp.
  • A content system behind the ads: scripts per service, doctor reels, UGC and AI-assisted videos, tested and refreshed every month.
  • A "winners" campaign that only runs proven creatives. It reached 13.97 QAR per conversation, 37% under the account average.
  • Snapchat lead forms and TikTok messaging to reach younger patients.
