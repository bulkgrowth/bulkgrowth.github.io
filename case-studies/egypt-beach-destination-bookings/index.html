Result: in the first 3 months, EGP 11,430 of Meta Ads produced 2,372 booking messages and EGP 787,000 in revenue (client-reported) at an average ticket of EGP 250. To date the account has brought 12,626 conversations at EGP 1.82 each.

What we did

  • A full-funnel plan for both families (B2C) and companies booking group trips (B2B).
  • Messaging built around exclusivity and the family experience.
  • UGC and drone videos of the beach, tested against each other.
  • Messaging campaigns for bookings, with KPIs tracked weekly.
