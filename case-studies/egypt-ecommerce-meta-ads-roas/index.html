Result: EGP 1,736,750 of spend produced 10,879 purchases worth EGP 10,603,545, a blended 6.1x ROAS. A campaign spending over EGP 100,000 still returned 10.56x.

What we did

  • Purchase tracking first: Pixel and Conversions API sending purchase value.
  • Broad targeting with layered retargeting, which scaled better than narrow interests.
  • Creative rotation across static, carousel and video.
  • Bid and budget control to hold ROAS as spend grew.
