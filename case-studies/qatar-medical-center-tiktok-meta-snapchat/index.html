Case study · Medical center

30,000+ patient conversations across Meta, TikTok and Snapchat

A medical and aesthetic center in Qatar that needed more patient inquiries than one platform could deliver.

Result: 26,249 conversations from Meta at 19.42 QAR, 4,133 from TikTok at 13.92 QAR, and 410 Snapchat leads at about $6.66 each. TikTok delivered conversations 28% cheaper than Meta for the same clinic and offers.

The challenge

Meta was the main source of patients, but costs rose as budgets grew. The center needed new channels that could bring the same kind of patient without raising the cost per inquiry.

What we did

  • Kept Meta as the base with a long-running messaging campaign and monthly treatment offers.
  • Opened TikTok with native, doctor-led videos and before-and-after style hooks (within platform rules).
  • Tested Snapchat lead forms for younger Gulf audiences.
  • Compared every channel on the same metric: cost per conversation or lead.

Takeaway

For clinics in the Gulf, TikTok is no longer just an awareness channel. With native creative it brought patient conversations cheaper than Meta. See how we split budgets between channels.
