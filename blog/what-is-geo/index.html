What is GEO, and how do brands get recommended by ChatGPT?

People now ask AI assistants which product, clinic or agency to choose. GEO is how you make sure your brand is part of that answer.

By Mohamed Elmlegey · Published 2026-10-14 · 6 min read

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of making your brand easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand, trust and cite. It builds on SEO, and focuses on clear, quotable answers, structured data, consistent business information and mentions on sources AI models rely on.

How AI assistants decide what to say

When someone asks an AI assistant a question, it combines what it learned in training with pages it retrieves from the web at that moment. Brands that appear in its answer are usually the ones that are easy to find, clearly described and mentioned by other trusted sources.

GEO vs SEO

SEOGEO
GoalRank in search resultsBe mentioned or cited in AI answers
UnitA pageA passage, fact or brand mention
Measured byRankings, clicksMentions, citations, AI referral traffic
Shared baseCrawlable site, clear content, trust signals

Six things that improve GEO

  1. Answer-first writing. Start pages with a short, self-contained answer that could be quoted on its own.
  2. Clear entity information. The same business name, services, location and contact details everywhere.
  3. Structured data. Organization, Service, FAQ and Article schema that matches what's on the page.
  4. Crawlable pages. Content in plain HTML, a sitemap, and robots.txt that doesn't block AI search crawlers.
  5. Mentions elsewhere. Reviews, directories, interviews and articles on sites AI tools read.
  6. Specific, original content. Real processes, examples and numbers that generic pages don't have.

How to measure GEO

  • Run the same set of buyer questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity every month and record whether your brand appears.
  • Track referral traffic from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.com in GA4.
  • Watch branded searches in Search Console. AI mentions often lead to people searching your name.

What GEO can't do

No one can guarantee that an AI assistant will recommend a brand. GEO removes the reasons you're left out and makes you a stronger candidate. Learn more about our SEO and GEO service, or reach people inside ChatGPT today with ChatGPT Ads.

Frequently asked questions

Is GEO different from SEO?

GEO builds on SEO. SEO targets rankings in search results; GEO targets being mentioned or cited inside AI-generated answers. Most of the groundwork is shared.

Should I block AI crawlers?

For a business that wants to be recommended, no. Blocking AI search crawlers makes it harder for AI assistants to read and cite your site.
